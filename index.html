To My Beautiful Baby, ❤️

Happy four months, my love.

Four months ago, our story began, and I never imagined how much happiness one person could bring into my life.

Even with an entire country between us, you became such an important part of my days.

I loved talking to you, laughing with you, and finding little ways to feel close even when we were far apart.

And then you came to Mexico.

I still remember the moment I finally held you in my arms. It was something I had dreamed about for so long, and suddenly it was real.

Those days together gave me memories I will always treasure. Not because everything had to be perfect, but because I was sharing those moments with you.

Now that we're apart again, I miss being close to you. I miss the hugs, the little moments, and simply having you beside me.

I know that traveling to Canada and seeing you again will take time. I can't promise an exact date, and I don't want to make promises I can't yet keep.

But I want you to know that seeing you again is something I truly hope and want to work toward.

Thank you for these four months, for the memories we've made, and for being part of this story with me.

I hope this is only the beginning of many more beautiful chapters together.

Happy four months, my beautiful baby.
I love you more than words can express. ❤️
